How Dusra Brain works with a decision
Dusra Brain first understands the situation, checks what you know, helps structure the choices, verifies the support behind the analysis, and keeps a record of decisions you explicitly confirm. Technical details remain available inside each card.
Understand the situation
Dusra Brain identifies what you are trying to decide, what you want to achieve, and what information is available.
Explore choices
When there is enough information, Dusra Brain can compare the choices, benefits, risks and trade-offs you have provided.
Record and revisit your decisions
You are always in control. Dusra Brain only records a decision when you explicitly confirm it, and it can later show the history and evidence behind it.
See how decisions change over time
Dusra Brain can connect past decisions with current plans, identify possible changes or conflicts, and explain the evidence behind those changes.
What happened after your decision?
When you have a real outcome to report, tell Dusra Brain what happened. Nothing is inferred from your plans, memories or conversations.
